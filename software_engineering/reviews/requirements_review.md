## Summary

The draft is strong. It has a clear structure, honest gap notes, a good risk register, and most "Implemented" claims are accurate. The main problems:

1. **Some statuses overstate the code.** 9 "Implemented" items are really Partial: CFG-005, REG-007, MED-005, ART-002, ORC-004, GH-004, NFR-REL-002, NFR-SCL-001 and NFR-CMP-002. The worst are the article crawler's copied Chrome User-Agent and the Medium setup, which waits for a person to click a bot challenge. Both are presented as compliant.
2. **Multi-user correctness gaps that are cheap to fix now.** An owned source's `owner_id` and a shared source's topics and `enabled` flag are last-writer-wins. This enables an ownership takeover (findings 1 and 2).
3. **The chains between stages are missing.** There is no contract from the ETL to the feature pipeline, no delete operation in the store, no way to restore a snapshot, and no unattended mode. Several Planned items therefore depend on things that do not exist (findings 7, 9, 10, 11).
4. **Missing requirements:** UI authentication and account lifecycle, consent, proof of account ownership, cost limits outside dataset generation and training, backups, a golden evaluation set, and a grounded "I don't know" behaviour.
5. **No priorities or phases**, a few duplicates, broken cross-references, and about a dozen acceptance criteria that could not be tested.

**Change counts (functional requirements):** 129 in the draft → 162 IDs in v1.0. **33 added** and **1 withdrawn** (FR-TRG-001). **8 FRs re-statused** from Implemented (CFG-005, REG-007, MED-005, ART-002, ORC-004 and GH-004 to Partial; LI-002 to the new "Implemented (unverified)"; MED-003 kept Implemented but put on compliance hold). Non-functional requirements: **4 added** (REL-007, MNT-006, COST-001, COST-002), **1 withdrawn** (REL-003), **4 re-statused** (REL-002, SCL-001 and CMP-002 to Partial; PRIV-002 from the undefined "Open" to "Planned, baseline violates it"). Every FR now has a Priority and a Phase; every NFR has a separate Status and Phase.

**Severity scale:** **Critical** = would ship a wrong or unsafe system if followed as written; **High** = a wrong status on a Must item, or a missing link that blocks a phase; **Medium** = a testability, consistency or completeness gap that would cause rework; **Low** = editorial or minor accuracy.

No finding was rated Critical. The baseline is single-user and local, so none of the issues can cause harm yet. Findings 1, 6 and 8 would become Critical in P4.
